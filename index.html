AI and the Online Health Information Market

Samira Gholami

Project Description and Context

This project studies how generative AI is changing the online health information ecosystem. As more users turn to AI tools for direct health answers, traditional health websites may lose visits and ad revenue. Because medically reviewed content is costly to produce and maintain, this shift raises concerns about long-run incentives to provide reliable public-facing information.

Short Literature Summary

  • Health Information and AI Adoption: prior work examines quality, trust, and user behavior when people rely on AI versus conventional health sources.
  • AI as a Digital Intermediary: recent studies analyze whether AI substitutes for search and changes how users reach online information.
  • Supply-Side Sustainability: emerging evidence highlights risks to publisher incentives from traffic erosion and AI content extraction.

Research Question

How does AI reshape the online health information market on both the demand side (user browsing and substitution behavior) and the supply side (website traffic, crawler blocking, and content strategy)?
